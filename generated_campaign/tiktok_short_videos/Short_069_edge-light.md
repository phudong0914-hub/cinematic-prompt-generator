Ridley Scott and his frequent cinematographer John Mathieson use edge lighting extensively in "Gladiator" and "Kingdom of Heaven" to make armored warriors pop against dark battle backgrounds. Roger Deakins uses hairline edge lights in "Blade Runner 2049" where characters are often defined more by their luminous outlines than their illuminated faces. The technique is also fundamental to music video and commercial cinematography where separation and visual polish are paramount."
