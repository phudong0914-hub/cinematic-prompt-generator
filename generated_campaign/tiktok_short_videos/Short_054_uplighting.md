Murnau's "Nosferatu" employed underlighting to transform Max Schreck into a figure of pure dread. Modern horror directors like Ari Aster use subtle uplighting in "Hereditary" during the séance sequences, and Jordan Peele employs it in "Us" when the tethered versions of characters emerge from below."
