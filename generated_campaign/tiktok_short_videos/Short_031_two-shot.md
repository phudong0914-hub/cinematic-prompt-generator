### 🎬 KỊCH BẢN THỜI LƯỢNG 30 GIÂY (TIKTOK / SHORTS / REELS):
1. **0s - 3s (VIRAL HOOK):** "Bạn có biết bí mật tạo nên góc quay Two-Shot như phim Hollywood không?"
2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "A shot framing exactly two subjects, showing their spatial and emotional relationship, essential for establishing dynamics between characters in conversation, confrontation, or intimacy. Billy Wilder was a master of the two-shot, using it in "The Apartment" and "Some Like It Hot" to capture the chemistry of his actors. Before Midnight director Richard Linklater builds entire films from two-shots of couples walking and talking, and Wong Kar-wai uses cramped two-shots in "In the Mood for Love" to convey forbidden intimacy within claustrophobic spaces."
3. **15s - 25s (PROMPT MẪU):** Dán đoạn prompt dưới đây vào Midjourney / Sora / NotebookLM:

```text
Two-shot of [Subject] framed together, their bodies creating complementary angles, the space between them charged with everything said and unsaid, shot on a 50mm Summilux at f/1.4 with gentle background blur, Kodak Portra 400 color rendition with soft warm midtones, the quiet eloquence of two figures in shared space
```
