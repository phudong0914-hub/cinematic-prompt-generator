The classic Hollywood musical relied on high-key lighting — Vincente Minnelli's "An American in Paris" and "The Band Wagon" glow with uniform brightness. Kubrick used clinical high-key lighting in the space station sequences of "2001" to create sterile futurism, and Sofia Coppola bathes "Marie Antoinette" in high-key pastel light to capture the candy-colored excess of Versailles. The technique is also fundamental to the visual language of romantic comedies from Nora Ephron to Nancy Meyers."
