2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "A sequence of short shots edited together to compress time, convey information, or build emotional momentum — from training sequences to falling-in-love sequences, montage is cinema's time machine. Sergei Eisenstein theorized montage as cinema's unique art form in the 1920s, and his Odessa Steps sequence in "Battleship Potemkin" remains the most studied montage in film history. Rocky Balboa's training montage set to "Gonna Fly Now" defined the modern montage for a generation. Martin Scorsese uses montage in "Goodfellas" to compress years of criminal excess into exhilarating minutes, and Edgar Wright creates kinetic comic montages in the Cornetto trilogy."
