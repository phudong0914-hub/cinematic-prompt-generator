2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "A horizontal rotation of the camera on a fixed axis, sweeping left or right to reveal the breadth of a space, follow lateral movement, or connect subjects across a scene. John Ford's slow, reverent pans across Monument Valley in "The Searchers" established the landscape as a character. Jean Renoir pioneered fluid panning in "The Rules of the Game," and Paul Thomas Anderson uses methodical lateral pans in "There Will Be Blood" to survey the oil fields with the deliberate gaze of a prospector scanning for fortune."
