2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "Shooting with only the light naturally present in the location — no artificial movie lights added — creating an authentic, documentary quality that requires careful exposure management. Kubrick's "Barry Lyndon" is the most famous example, shot entirely by candlelight and window light using a modified NASA f/0.7 Zeiss lens. Emmanuel Lubezki committed to available light for Terrence Malick's "The New World" and "The Tree of Life," as well as Iñárritu's "The Revenant," winning three consecutive Oscars for his mastery of natural illumination. Bradford Young's available-light work in "Arrival" created an intimate, naturalistic atmosphere within science fiction."
