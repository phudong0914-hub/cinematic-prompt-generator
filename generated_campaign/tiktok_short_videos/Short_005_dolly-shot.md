2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "A smooth camera movement where the entire camera physically moves toward, away from, or alongside the subject on a wheeled platform or track, creating an immersive sense of movement through space. Orson Welles used dolly shots to navigate the deep-focus interiors of "Citizen Kane," while Spike Lee invented his signature double-dolly shot — mounting both actor and camera on the same platform — to create a floating, surreal glide seen in "Do the Right Thing" and "25th Hour." Martin Scorsese's famous Copacabana shot in "Goodfellas" tracks Henry Hill through the back entrance of a nightclub in one fluid dolly movement."
