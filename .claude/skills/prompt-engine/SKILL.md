# 🎬 Cine Prompt Engineering Skill

Use this skill when modifying or expanding prompt generation logic, adding new AI video/image models, or refining prompt token weights.

## 4-Layer Director Pipeline Architecture
1. **Layer 1: Optical Core & Resolution**
   - 4K/8K UHD, anamorphic lens optics (e.g. ARRI Alexa 65, Panavision C-Series, Cook anamorphic).
   - Shutter angle (180-degree motion blur), grain structure (Kodak 5219 500T).
2. **Layer 2: Shot Framing & Camera Motion**
   - Framing: Extreme Close-Up (ECU), Medium Close-Up (MCU), Cowboy Shot, Drone Orbit, Whip Pan.
   - Movements: Dutch angle, tracking dolly, crane boom, push-in zoom.
3. **Layer 3: Lighting & Color Grading**
   - Cinematic palettes: Chiaroscuro, rim lighting, volumetric god rays, tungsten warm / sodium vapor.
   - Presets: Deakins Naturalistic, Wong Kar-wai Neon Melancholy, Villeneuve Desert Monochromatic.
4. **Layer 4: Foley & Audio Ducking**
   - Synchronized audio cues, localized dialogue scripts, orchestral ducking.

## Target Model Formats
- **Midjourney v8**: Parameters `--ar 16:9 --style raw --v 8.0 --c 5`.
- **Google Veo 3**: Native natural-language camera directives with duration and motion speed.
- **Wan 2.5 / Sora**: Scene continuity markers, physics consistency cues.
- **Kling / Runway Gen-3**: Structural camera movement tags (`[Pan Left]`, `[Camera Zoom In]`).
