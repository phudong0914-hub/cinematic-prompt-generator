### 🎬 KỊCH BẢN THỜI LƯỢNG 30 GIÂY (TIKTOK / SHORTS / REELS):
1. **0s - 3s (VIRAL HOOK):** "Bạn có biết bí mật tạo nên góc quay Eye-Level Shot như phim Hollywood không?"
2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "A neutral shot taken at the subject's eye height, the most natural and common camera angle, creating a sense of equality and objectivity between viewer and subject. Yasujiro Ozu famously placed his camera at a low eye-level (the "tatami shot") in films like "Tokyo Story," creating an intimate, respectful perspective that defined Japanese domestic cinema. The Dardenne brothers use persistent eye-level handheld work in "Rosetta" and "The Child" to maintain unflinching equality with their working-class subjects, never looking down on or up at them."
3. **15s - 25s (PROMPT MẪU):** Dán đoạn prompt dưới đây vào Midjourney / Sora / NotebookLM:

```text
Eye-level shot of [Subject] with the camera positioned exactly at their eye height creating a direct and equal relationship with the viewer, natural available light providing gentle directional illumination, no dramatic angles or manipulation, shot on 16mm film stock with gentle grain, naturalistic color palette, intimate documentary stillness
```
