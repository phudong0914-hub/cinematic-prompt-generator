2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "A scene that takes the audience back to an earlier point in time, revealing backstory, providing context for present behavior, or recontextualizing what we thought we knew. "Citizen Kane" is structured entirely around flashbacks as reporters investigate Charles Foster Kane's life. Francis Ford Coppola's "The Godfather Part II" masterfully interweaves flashbacks of young Vito Corleone with the present-day story of his son Michael. Christopher Nolan uses fragmented flashbacks as a structural principle in "Memento," where the reversed chronology makes every flashback a revelation. Terrence Malick's "The Tree of Life" uses flashback as pure sensory memory, evoking childhood through images rather than plot."
