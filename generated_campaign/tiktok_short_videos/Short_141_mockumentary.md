Rob Reiner's "This Is Spinal Tap" (1984) established the mockumentary as a legitimate comedic form. Christopher Guest continued the tradition with "Waiting for Guffman," "Best in Show," and "A Mighty Wind." Ricky Gervais and Stephen Merchant's "The Office" (UK) made the mockumentary format a television staple, leading to the American version and eventually "Parks and Recreation" and "Modern Family." Taika Waititi's "What We Do in the Shadows" brought the mockumentary to horror-comedy."
