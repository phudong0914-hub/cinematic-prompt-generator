2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "A shot where the subject moves or faces directly toward the camera, creating a confrontational, powerful feeling as the subject approaches or stares directly at the viewer. Stanley Kubrick mastered the head-on shot with his famous "Kubrick stare" — characters like Alex in "A Clockwork Orange" and Jack Torrance in "The Shining" glaring directly into the lens with menacing intensity. Spike Lee's double-dolly head-on shots place characters in direct communion with the audience, while Wes Anderson uses symmetrical head-on framing as a core visual signature in every film."
