2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "A shot where the camera is tilted on its roll axis, creating a diagonal horizon line to convey unease, disorientation, tension, or a character's disturbed psychological state. Carol Reed made the Dutch angle iconic in "The Third Man" (1949), tilting nearly every frame in the Vienna sewers to mirror the moral corruption of Harry Lime. Tim Burton adopted it as a signature style in "Batman" and "Edward Scissorhands," while Kenneth Branagh used it relentlessly in "Thor" to evoke the comic-book panels of Jack Kirby."
