## 🌟 Overview

**Cine Prompt Pro v2.0** is an enterprise-grade prompt engineering and automated cinematic directing suite. Built with the **3-Column IPO Architecture** (Input → Process → Output) and adhering to the Steve Jobs philosophy of extreme simplicity and craftsmanship, it empowers creators, directors, and marketers to generate Hollywood-grade prompts with deep optical and acoustic fidelity.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **🧠 4-Layer Master Director Engine (`aiService.js`):**
  - **Layer 1:** System, Quality & Resolution (4K UHD, HDR10, 24fps, Color Consistency).
  - **Layer 2:** Scene 1 (Hook) — Wide-angle optics, volumetric lighting, motion dynamics.
  - **Layer 3:** Scene 2 (Medium) & Scene 3 (Close-up) — Character micro-expressions, bokeh, focal racking.
  - **Layer 4:** Audio & Foley Engine — Native Vietnamese voiceover scripts, ambient soundscapes, orchestral audio ducking.
- **🎬 20 Hand-Crafted AI Video Styles:**
  - Cinematic Masterpiece, National Geographic Documentary, Film Noir, Cyberpunk Neon Noir, Sci-Fi Blockbuster (Dune/Interstellar), Epic Fantasy, Anime (Makoto Shinkai), Spider-Verse Comic, Psychological Horror (A24), Drone FPV, and more.
- **⚡ Multi-Model AI Hub:**
  - Direct integration with OpenRouter, Gemini, Groq, Ollama, OpenAI, DeepSeek, and custom BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) rotation with secure client-side encryption.
- **🌉 PromptAgent Flow Bridge Pro:**
  - Seamless 1-Click relay and bidirectional cross-platform pipeline integration with Remotion, Hypit, Powtoon, and automated video workflows.
- **🔒 Security Shield & Optical Linter:**
  - Real-time client-side prompt validation, optical syntax checks, anti-injection guardrails, and secure export (PDF, CSV, Video Packages).

## 🌐 Deploy to Vercel (1-Click)

This repository is pre-configured with `vercel.json` for instant static hosting:

1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and log in with your GitHub account.
2. Click **Add New...** → **Project**.
3. Select this repository: `phudong0914-hub/cinematic-prompt-generator`.
4. Framework Preset: Choose **Other** (Root directory: `./`).
5. Click **Deploy**. Your app will be live globally in under 10 seconds!
