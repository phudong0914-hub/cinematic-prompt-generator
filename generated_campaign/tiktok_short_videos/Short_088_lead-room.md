### 🎬 KỊCH BẢN THỜI LƯỢNG 30 GIÂY (TIKTOK / SHORTS / REELS):
1. **0s - 3s (VIRAL HOOK):** "Bạn có biết bí mật tạo nên góc quay Lead Room như phim Hollywood không?"
2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "Empty space in front of a moving subject or in the direction they're looking, giving the subject visual breathing room and implying destination or intent. Lead room is one of the fundamental principles of shot composition — violating it deliberately creates tension and unease, as the Coen Brothers and David Fincher sometimes do in thriller sequences. Denis Villeneuve uses generous lead room in "Arrival" to suggest the vastness of the unknown that Amy Adams's character faces. Breaking the convention — placing a character at the leading edge of the frame with nothing ahead — immediately signals to the audience that something is wrong."
3. **15s - 25s (PROMPT MẪU):** Dán đoạn prompt dưới đây vào Midjourney / Sora / NotebookLM:

```text
Lead room composition with [Subject] positioned on one side of the frame with generous empty space in the direction of their gaze or movement, the open space suggesting possibility and destination, the satisfying rightness of proper lead room creating visual momentum, shot on a 50mm lens, the composition feeling natural and kinetically forward
```
