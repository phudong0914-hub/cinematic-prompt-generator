2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "The perceptual relationship between a subject (figure) and its background (ground) — strong figure-ground separation makes subjects pop, while ambiguous relationships create artistic tension. Film noir deliberately plays with figure-ground by merging characters into shadows, while Spielberg ensures crisp separation for visual clarity. Kubrick uses monochromatic figure-ground merging in "Full Metal Jacket" to show soldiers losing individuality. Roger Deakins creates separation through subtle lighting rather than color contrast, and cinematographer James Laxton uses luminous skin against dark backgrounds in "Moonlight" and "If Beale Street Could Talk" to celebrate Black skin tones."
