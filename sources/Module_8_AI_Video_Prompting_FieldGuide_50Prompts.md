# 🎬 CINE PROMPT PRO - HỌC PHẦN 8: CẨM NANG VIẾT PROMPT VIDEO AI (4 LỚP & 50 MẪU THỰC HÀNH)

> Tài liệu chuẩn nạp vào NotebookLM Video Overviews. Hướng dẫn viết Prompt 4 lớp (Camera + Scene + Action + Details), 5 Bộ Khóa Nhất Quán (Character, Environment, Camera, Orientation, Dialogue) và 50 Mẫu Prompt Thực Hành cho Veo 3.1, Gemini Omni, Runway Gen-3.

---

## 📌 1. CÔNG THỨC 4 LỚP NỀN TẢNG (4-LAYER FORMULA)

```text
[CAMERA] + [SCENE] + [ACTION] + [DETAILS]
```
- **Lớp 1 (Camera):** Chọn 1 chuyển động máy quay chính (Slow dolly forward, side tracking shot, static locked shot...).
- **Lớp 2 (Scene):** Nơi chốn + Thời gian + Nguồn sáng + Thời tiết/Bối cảnh.
- **Lớp 3 (Action):** Một chủ thể chính thực hiện 1 hành động trung tâm duy nhất (trong 5-10s).
- **Lớp 4 (Details):** Tốc độ, chất lượng chuyển động, âm thanh môi trường hoặc câu thoại Tiếng Việt (Lip-sync).

---

## 🔒 2. 5 BỘ KHÓA NHẤT QUÁN (CONSISTENCY LOCKS CHO VIDEO NHIỀU CẢNH)
1. **Character Lock:** Tuổi, giới tính, dân tộc, trang phục, màu da, kiểu tóc.
2. **Environment Lock:** Địa điểm, thời gian, nguồn sáng, vật thể nền không đổi.
3. **Camera Lock:** Hướng nhìn, chiều cao camera, chuyển động chính của từng shot.
4. **Orientation Lock:** Nhân vật quay mặt hướng nào, vị trí trái/phải trong khung hình.
5. **Dialogue Lock:** Ai nói, ai im lặng, ngôn ngữ, câu thoại chính xác và lip-sync.

## 🎥 3. TRỌN BỘ 50 PROMPT MẪU THỰC HÀNH (COPY & NẠP NOTEBOOKLM)

### A. CINEMATIC MOOD - CẢNH ĐIỆN ẢNH (1-5)
1. **Cửa sổ xe buýt lúc mưa:**
   `Slow push-in through a rain-speckled bus window at blue hour. A Vietnamese man in his thirties sits alone by the glass, watching city lights blur across his reflection. Warm interior light contrasts with the cool street outside, gentle engine hum and rain tapping the window.`
2. **Bình minh trên ruộng bậc thang:**
   `Drone rise from a single farmer standing beside a narrow path in Vietnamese rice terraces at sunrise. The camera climbs slowly to reveal layered fields wrapped in morning mist. Soft golden light spreads across the hills, distant birds call from the valley.`
3. **Hành lang sau sân khấu:**
   `Steady handheld follow through a narrow backstage corridor before a live performance. A young singer walks toward a bright stage entrance holding a small microphone, cables and crew members softly out of focus. Footsteps, muffled crowd energy and a low room tone build anticipation.`
4. **Bàn ăn sau bữa tối:**
   `Locked-off medium shot of a modest family dining room late at night. A woman slowly folds a child's paper drawing and places it beside a warm table lamp. Empty bowls, quiet ceiling-fan movement and distant neighborhood sounds create a tender reflective mood.`
5. **Vòng quanh chiếc đèn cổ:**
   `Slow orbit around an antique brass lantern on a wooden table inside an old riverside house at dusk. Dust particles drift through a narrow beam of window light while the flame flickers gently inside the glass. Soft wood creaks and evening insects fill the room.`

### B. PRODUCT & BEAUTY - SẢN PHẨM & LÀM ĐẸP (6-10)
6. **Serum trên đá ướt:**

7. **Nước hoa trong ánh hổ phách:**
   `Slow controlled rotation of an elegant perfume bottle on a matte black pedestal. Amber light passes through the glass and creates a warm reflection on the surface. A single cream-colored flower rests in the background, luxury studio styling, restrained cinematic sound bed.`
8. **Cà phê buổi sáng:**
   `High-speed close-up of dark coffee pouring into a white ceramic cup beside a sunlit kitchen window. The stream forms a small spiral, steam rises into the golden light, and a linen cloth moves softly in the morning breeze. Rich liquid texture, calm household ambience.`
9. **Giày chạm mặt đường:**
   `Low tracking shot following a limited-edition running shoe as it lands on a rain-darkened city pavement. A clean crown of water rises around the sole, droplets catch the late-afternoon light, and the camera keeps the side profile sharp while the street remains softly blurred.`
10. **Túi xách trong studio vải:**
    `Gentle crane down toward a structured leather handbag centered on a cream fabric pedestal. The camera settles as soft side light reveals stitching and grain across the surface. A subtle breeze lifts the background fabric, creating a refined editorial beauty campaign mood.`

### C. SOCIAL & TRANSFORMATION - SOCIAL & BIẾN ĐỔI (11-15)

Her light summer outfit smoothly transforms into layered autumn clothing with a wool coat and ankle boots while her pose and camera position remain consistent. Soft studio lighting, clean fashion editorial finish.`
12. **Phòng ngủ đổi phong cách:**
    `Slow lateral slide across a small bedroom as the space gradually changes from a cluttered student room into a calm minimalist bedroom. The bed and window stay in the same position while colors, storage and textiles become warmer and more organized. Gentle morning light remains consistent.`
13. **Bàn làm việc từ rối thành gọn:**
    `Locked overhead shot of a wooden desk covered with scattered notes, cables and stationery. In a smooth accelerated transformation, the objects align into a clean work setup with a laptop, notebook and small plant. The final arrangement holds under soft daylight with subtle paper sounds.`
14. **Biến đổi kiểu tóc:**
    `Close-up portrait of a Vietnamese woman with shoulder-length straight hair in soft studio light. Her hairstyle smoothly changes into a short textured bob while her face, head angle and neutral expression remain stable. Clean beauty lighting, detailed hair strands, polished transformation timing.`
15. **Gương mở sang thế giới khác:**
    `Slow dolly forward toward a person standing before an old oval mirror in a dim room. As the camera reaches the reflection, the mirror surface opens into a sunlit seaside landscape with moving clouds and bright water. The person stays still, warm room tone blending into ocean wind.`

### D. CHARACTER PERFORMANCE - NHÂN VẬT HÀNH ĐỘNG (16-20)
16. **Người bán hoa cắt cành:**

17. **Barista rót hình trái tim:**
    `Close-up tracking shot beside a barista pouring steamed milk into a white ceramic cup. The milk forms a clear heart shape on the coffee surface while the barista's hands remain steady. Warm cafe light, espresso machine hiss and quiet conversation in the background.`
18. **Nghệ sĩ vẽ nét cuối:**
    `Static medium shot of a Vietnamese illustrator at a wooden desk in a sunlit studio. The illustrator adds one final blue brushstroke to a large paper landscape and pauses to look at the finished image. Bristles move softly, paper texture visible, calm room ambience.`
19. **Đứa trẻ thả máy bay giấy:**
    `Gentle side tracking shot along a quiet school corridor after class. A child in a simple blue uniform releases one folded paper airplane toward an open window. The airplane glides through warm afternoon light, distant footsteps fade behind, hopeful cinematic atmosphere.`
20. **Nhạc công đánh hợp âm cuối:**
    `Slow push-in on a pianist's hands inside a nearly empty concert hall. The hands press one final chord on the grand piano and remain still as the sound echoes through the room. A single overhead spotlight, visible sheet music and a long natural decay.`

### E. NATURE & TIME-LAPSE - THIÊN NHIÊN & THỜI GIAN (21-25)
21. **Giọt nước trên lá:**
    `Extreme macro static shot of a fresh green leaf in a humid forest after rain. A water droplet slowly gathers at the tip, grows heavier and falls into the dark soil below. Soft backlight reveals the droplet edge, distant insects and gentle leaf movement.`
22. **Sương tràn qua ruộng:**
    `Time-lapse from a locked wide frame over terraced rice fields at dawn. Thin white fog flows slowly across the valley and settles between the green layers while the sky shifts from gray-blue to pale gold. A quiet morning soundscape supports the gradual reveal.`
23. **Nấm mọc trên nền rêu:**
    `Macro time-lapse on a moss-covered forest floor. A small amber mushroom pushes upward through wet leaves and gradually opens its cap. Tiny droplets remain on the moss, diffused forest light stays constant, humid atmosphere and soft woodland ambience.`
24. **Rừng tảo dưới biển:**
    `Slow underwater tracking shot gliding between tall kelp in clear blue water. Sunbeams descend from the surface as small silver fish pass through the moving leaves. Fine particles drift in the current, bubbles rise gently, immersive underwater ambience.`
25. **Hoa hướng dương nở:**
    `Time-lapse close-up of a closed sunflower bud in a quiet field. The green outer leaves loosen and the yellow petals unfold toward the morning sun. The background remains softly blurred while a light breeze moves the stem and distant bees approach.`

### F. TRANSITIONS & EFFECTS - CHUYỂN CẢNH & HIỆU ỨNG (26-30)
26. **Đồng xu thành bánh xe:**
    `Static close-up of a silver coin spinning above a wooden table. At the highest point of its rotation, the circular motion continues seamlessly as the coin becomes a polished bicycle wheel turning beside a sunlit road. Clean match cut, crisp metallic sound, bright afternoon energy.`
27. **Cánh cửa qua thời đại:**
    `Slow dolly forward through a wooden doorway in a 1920s Vietnamese house. The camera passes the doorframe and emerges into a contemporary bright living room while the walking figure keeps the same direction and pace. Dusty warm light shifts into clean daylight.`
28. **Màu sơn quét thành phong cảnh:**
    `Macro tracking shot following a thick brush of cobalt blue paint across white paper. As the brush reaches the edge of the frame, the blue stroke expands into a wide ocean horizon with sunlight moving across the water. Wet brush texture transitions into gentle waves.`
29. **Mắt mở ra vũ trụ:**
    `Extreme close-up slowly pushing toward a calm brown eye in soft light. The camera enters the dark pupil and reveals a vast field of stars with a distant nebula drifting across the frame. The eye reflection becomes the first star, seamless optical transition.`
30. **Hạt bồ công anh thành đàn chim:**
    `Static upward-looking shot of a hand holding a white dandelion against a clear blue sky. A gentle breath releases the seeds, and the floating white particles gradually transform into a small flock of birds crossing the sunlight. Soft wind, natural organic transformation.`

### G. DIALOGUE & LIP-SYNC - THOẠI & KHẨU HÌNH TIẾNG VIỆT (31-35)
31. **Hai người nói chuyện trong quán cà phê:**
    `Medium two-shot of two Vietnamese friends sitting side by side at a quiet cafe table. The woman on the left speaks one clear sentence: "I finally know what I want to build." The man on the right listens silently and does not move his lips. Warm window light, natural lip-sync, no overlapping dialogue, gentle cafe ambience.`
32. **Chuyên gia Skincare giải thích:**
    `Clean medium close-up of a Vietnamese female skincare advisor in her thirties, wearing a white blazer, speaking directly to camera. She says exactly: "The biggest mistake is using too many active ingredients at once." Precise English lip-sync, calm confident delivery, one continuous shot, soft clinical studio light.`
33. **Mẹ và con gái nói chuyện Tiếng Việt:**
    `Locked medium two-shot of a Vietnamese mother and her adult daughter sitting shoulder to shoulder on a sofa. The daughter turns slightly toward her mother and says exactly: "Mẹ đừng lo, con đã chuẩn bị rồi." The mother remains silent with a small reassuring smile. Accurate Vietnamese lip-sync, minimal hand movement, warm living-room ambience.`
34. **Phỏng vấn người bán hoa:**
    `Handheld documentary medium shot at a small morning flower stall. An interviewer holds a microphone toward an elderly Vietnamese flower seller. The seller looks at the interviewer and says exactly: "These flowers open best in the early morning." Only the seller speaks, natural pauses, realistic lip-sync.`
35. **Lời nhắn trong phòng thu:**
    `Slow push-in toward a Vietnamese man recording a short voice message inside a small sound booth. He looks into the microphone and says exactly: "If you can hear this, I made it home safely." His mouth matches every word, the camera never cuts, a faint headphone cable moves, intimate room tone.`

### H. SOUND-FIRST - ÂM THANH DẪN ĐƯỜNG HÌNH ẢNH (36-40)
36. **Sấm vang trước tia chớp:**
    `Locked-off interior shot of a small room during a night storm. A sudden deep thunder crack hits first, just before a bright lightning flash illuminates the walls and the person's startled face. Rain rattles the window continuously, realistic sound-to-picture timing.`
37. **Tiếng tàu đến trước khi xuất hiện:**
    `Wide shot of an empty rural railway crossing at dawn. The distant rumble and warning bell of an approaching train grow louder before the train enters the frame from behind the trees. Dust trembles beside the tracks, birds lift off only after the bell rings.`
38. **Ấm nước sôi trong bếp:**
    `Close-up of a stainless-steel kettle on a quiet kitchen stove. The rising whistle begins softly, grows sharper, and then the lid trembles as steam escapes. Only after the whistle peaks does a hand reach into frame to turn off the burner. Warm morning light.`
39. **Bước chân ngoài hành lang:**
    `Slow lateral tracking shot across a dim apartment hallway at night. Footsteps are heard approaching from outside the frame before a woman enters from the far doorway and stops beneath the ceiling light. The footsteps stop exactly when she stops, one continuous shot.`
40. **Sóng đập vào ghềnh đá:**
    `Low wide shot facing a dark coastal cliff at golden hour. The ocean swell becomes audible first, then a single wave crashes against the rocks and sends a fine spray into the sunlight. Gulls call afterward, the wind settles, slow cinematic camera drift.`

### I. IMAGE-TO-VIDEO & CONTINUITY - CHUYỂN ĐỘNG TỪ ẢNH (41-45)
41. **Chân dung có gió nhẹ:**
    `The camera performs a slow half-orbit around the person as she remains mostly still and blinks once. A gentle breeze moves only a few strands of hair and the fabric at her shoulder. Preserve the original face, outfit, lighting and background. Natural portrait motion, no cuts.`
42. **Sản phẩm studio:**
    `The camera slowly pushes in toward the product while two small water droplets slide down the glass surface. The background remains completely unchanged and softly out of focus. Preserve the exact product shape, label placement, color and studio lighting.`
43. **Nhân vật bước qua khung hình:**
    `The camera gently tracks left as the character walks at a slow, even pace from right to left. Keep the character's identity, hairstyle, clothing, scale and direction consistent with the reference image. Background objects remain stable, soft afternoon light.`
44. **Vòng lặp hoàn hảo (Loop):**
    `The locked-off camera remains perfectly still as the curtains move in a gentle repeating breeze. The scene must start and end on the exact same frame to create a seamless loop. Minimal subject motion only, stable lighting, no camera shake, no cuts.`
45. **Giữ bối cảnh, thay đổi hành động:**
    `The camera stays in a steady medium shot. Keep the person, outfit, room layout, lighting and framing exactly the same. Change only the action: she slowly places the notebook on the desk and looks toward the window. One continuous take, realistic hand movement.`

### J. OMNI EDIT & REVISION - CHỈNH SỬA NÂNG CAO (46-50)
46. **Đổi góc Over-the-shoulder:**
    `Keep the character, outfit, background, lighting and action exactly the same. Change only the camera angle to an over-the-shoulder close-up from behind the violinist, slowly pushing toward the moving bow. Preserve the original timing and audio.`
47. **Biến nền thành phố thành mưa:**
    `Keep the subject, face, clothing, pose and camera movement unchanged. Replace only the background with a rainy neon city street at night. Add realistic reflections on the pavement and distant traffic ambience, but do not add new people or change the subject's expression.`
48. **Chữ xuất hiện theo nhịp (Text-on-screen):**
    `Edit this video while keeping the visuals and camera movement unchanged. Add the exact phrase "MAKE IT CLEAR" in bold white sans-serif type at the lower center. Reveal one word at a time in sync with three soft percussive beats, with no other text on screen.`
49. **Đổi style đất sét (Stop-motion Claymation):**
    `Preserve the original subject motion, camera path, timing and composition. Reimagine the entire video as tactile stop-motion clay animation with visible fingerprints, warm tungsten light and a handmade miniature set. Do not add or remove any objects.`
50. **Kết hợp Video + Ảnh + Audio (Omni Multi-modal):**
