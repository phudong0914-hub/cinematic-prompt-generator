2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "Placing the subject dead center in the frame — when done deliberately, it creates a powerful, confrontational, or hypnotically ordered effect that requires confidence and intentionality. Wes Anderson builds his entire visual identity around centered subjects, creating his trademark "planimetric" compositions. Kubrick's centered one-point-perspective shots in "The Shining" and "Full Metal Jacket" use the center position for maximum psychological impact. Jonathan Demme's centered close-ups in "Silence of the Lambs" break the conventional off-center framing of dialogue scenes to create confrontational direct address."
