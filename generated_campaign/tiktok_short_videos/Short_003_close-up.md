2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "A tightly framed shot that fills the screen with a subject's face or a specific detail, revealing emotions, textures, and subtle details invisible in wider shots. Carl Theodor Dreyer's "The Passion of Joan of Arc" (1928) is built almost entirely from devastating close-ups of Renée Falconetti's face, widely considered the greatest performance ever captured on film. Sergio Leone elevated the close-up to operatic intensity in his Westerns, while Jonathan Demme's direct-to-camera close-ups in "The Silence of the Lambs" created unbearable intimacy with Hannibal Lecter."
