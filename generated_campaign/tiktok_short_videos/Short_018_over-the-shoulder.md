2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "Shot framed from behind one character, looking past their shoulder at another, the standard coverage for dialogue that creates spatial relationships and a sense of being within the conversation. The shot/reverse-shot pattern using over-the-shoulder angles became the backbone of Hollywood dialogue coverage through the classical studio era. David Fincher meticulously calibrates the exact angle and depth of his OTS shots in "The Social Network" and "Zodiac" to control psychological tension. Wong Kar-wai subverts the technique in "In the Mood for Love," using tight over-the-shoulder framings to suggest the suffocating proximity of secret desire."
