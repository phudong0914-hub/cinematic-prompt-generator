### 🎬 KỊCH BẢN THỜI LƯỢNG 30 GIÂY (TIKTOK / SHORTS / REELS):
1. **0s - 3s (VIRAL HOOK):** "Bạn có biết bí mật tạo nên góc quay Split Screen như phim Hollywood không?"
2. **3s - 15s (GIẢI THÍCH):** "The frame is divided into two or more sections, each showing a different angle, location, or timeline simultaneously, showing parallel action, phone conversations, or multiple perspectives at once. Brian De Palma made split screen his signature, using it in "Carrie," "Dressed to Kill," and "Snake Eyes" to create impossible simultaneity. Ang Lee used complex multi-panel split screens in "Hulk" to emulate comic book layouts. Denis Villeneuve employed split screen in "Enemy" to visualize duality, and the technique has experienced a revival in television through shows like "24" where real-time parallel action demanded simultaneous visual presentation."
3. **15s - 25s (PROMPT MẪU):** Dán đoạn prompt dưới đây vào Midjourney / Sora / NotebookLM:

```text
Split screen dividing the frame with [Subject] visible in both halves simultaneously, one side warm-toned and the other cool-toned, the vertical divide becoming the physical and emotional distance, the De Palma technique of making the audience omniscient observers of parallel realities occupying the same frame
```
